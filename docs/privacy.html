Your data, explained

Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026 · Applies to OmniClick and this website

The short version: notes are saved in your browser. If you enable Drive sync, note files go directly to your own Google Drive. OmniClick does not send your notes, email contents, or audio attachments to a developer-operated server.

1. Who provides OmniClick

OmniClick is a Chrome extension maintained through the wisecrunch/OmniClick GitHub repository. In this policy, “OmniClick” refers to the extension and its project website. Contact the maintainer through the repository’s issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include note contents, email messages, passwords, tokens, or other private information.

2. Data the extension accesses and stores

These operations run in your browser; OmniClick does not upload your inbox or build a developer-side mailbox database.

3. Optional Google Drive sync

Drive sync starts only after you configure and connect it through Google’s authorization flow. OmniClick requests the limited drive.file permission, which permits access to files the app creates or that you explicitly authorize for the app; it does not request access to all unrelated files in your Drive.

Sync transfers note records directly between your browser and Google Drive over HTTPS. The visible OmniClick Notes folder contains current note JSON files and a Backups folder with saved versions. File names can include your mailbox address, email subject, and thread identifier. The note files themselves contain the note text and its metadata. Only notes for the connected Drive email address are synced; other mailboxes’ notes remain local.

OmniClick does not intentionally share these folders with other people. If you change their sharing permissions or export and share a backup, others may gain access. Google processes data held in your Drive according to your account settings and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Use and sharing of Google user data

Google user data is used only to provide the note, sync, backup, restore, and audio features you enable. OmniClick does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence models. The extension does not provide a developer-side service for reading your notes or email.

OmniClick’s handling of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is transferred to the Google services needed for the requested features; there is no additional developer-operated storage server or analytics recipient in this version.

5. Retention, deletion, and your controls

  • Local notes: remain in your browser profile until deleted or the extension’s data is removed. Uninstalling the extension normally removes its local storage. Export a backup first if you need to keep a copy.
  • Deleting a note: removes it from the active notes list. A deletion marker may remain locally and in Drive to prevent an older copy from another computer from reappearing. Earlier uploaded text remains in saved versions until those backup files are deleted.
  • Drive files and saved versions: remain in your Google Drive until you remove them. Disconnecting Drive or uninstalling OmniClick does not delete those files.
  • Stopping sync: use Disconnect in Drive settings. You can also revoke access through your Google account’s third-party connection settings.
  • Removing every copy: disconnect sync on all computers, remove local extension data on each profile, delete OmniClick’s Drive note and backup files, and remove any exported backups you no longer want. Drive’s own trash and retention rules still apply.

Turning off the notes feature hides its Gmail interface but does not delete notes or disconnect Drive. Removing a current file directly in Drive does not delete a local note; a connected installation may upload it again.

6. Security

Google requests use HTTPS. Drive authorization is managed through Chrome’s identity API, and the extension does not write access tokens into its note storage or source files. Notes are not separately encrypted by OmniClick before being placed in local storage or Drive. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile, your Google account, a shared note folder, or an exported backup may be able to read them.

7. Website hosting and support

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics, advertising scripts, forms, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process visitor request information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you submit a support issue, GitHub and the maintainer receive the information you choose to include. Avoid posting private email or note data. Information you publish in issues is separate from the extension’s local and Drive storage.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when OmniClick’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy, and changes are available in the repository history. Material changes involving additional Google data use will be disclosed before that new use and will require consent where applicable.

For privacy questions or a request concerning information you deliberately submitted to the project, contact the maintainer through GitHub Issues, without including the private information in the public request. The maintainer cannot directly delete notes held only in your browser or your private Drive; use the controls described above.
